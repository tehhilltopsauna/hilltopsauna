How to prepare

Your cacao ritual

1
Measure

Use 10–30g of cacao chips per serving, depending on how rich you like your drink.

2
Warm

Add to 150–200ml of warm water or a nut milk such as almond or oat (for better absorption of the minerals). Avoid boiling.

3
Whisk

Stir or whisk until smooth and creamy.

4
Make it yours

Add cinnamon, vanilla, chilli, honey, maple syrup or your favourite spices if desired.

5
Set your intention

Take a few slow breaths. Hold your cup to your heart, set an intention for your moment, and sip slowly.
