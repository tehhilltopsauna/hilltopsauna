Mums Morning

A dedicated weekly session just for mums — a proper hour away from the noise, the to-do lists and the demands of the week. Come on your own or bring a friend. The full facility is yours: sauna, cold plunge, hot tub and outdoor shower, with the Cavehill views thrown in.

It runs every Wednesday at 10am. Book your spot online and we'll see you on the hill.
